South India · Nilgiri Hills

Ooty

Known as the “Queen of Hill Stations,” Ooty is a peaceful escape nestled among the misty Nilgiri Mountains. Discover endless tea gardens, cool mountain air, scenic lakes, and charming colonial landscapes. Ride the iconic Nilgiri Mountain Railway, explore Doddabetta Peak, or simply unwind among the rolling green hills. Ooty offers a timeless mountain retreat for those seeking nature and tranquility.

  • Nilgiri Mountain Railway experiences
  • Scenic Ooty Lake & boating
  • Doddabetta Peak mountain views
  • Tea estate & plantation tours

South India · Heritage & Royality

Mysore

Step into the royal charm of Mysore, a city celebrated for magnificent palaces, historic architecture, vibrant culture, and beautiful gardens. Explore the grand Mysore Palace, climb the sacred Chamundi Hills, wander through Brindavan Gardens, or discover the city's rich heritage and traditional cuisine. Mysore blends regal elegance with the warmth of South Indian hospitality.

  • Grand Mysore Palace experiences
  • Chamundi Hills & temple visits
  • Brindavan Gardens evenings
  • Heritage walks & cultural experiences

South India · Tea & Misty Hills

Munnar

Nestled in the Western Ghats, Munnar is a serene mountain escape where endless tea plantations meet mist-covered hills and cool mountain air. Wander through emerald tea estates, explore the wildlife of Eravikulam National Park, cruise across Mattupetty Lake, or watch the sunset from Top Station. Munnar is the perfect retreat for reconnecting with nature and slowing down.

  • Munnar tea plantation experiences
  • Eravikulam National Park excursions
  • Mattupetty Dam & boating
  • Top Station mountain viewpoints
